02 / HOW I THINK

Good design is a series of thoughtful decisions.

My engineering background taught me to break down complex systems. I’m learning to apply that same care to the way people experience them.

01
▤

Make the hierarchy obvious.

Use layout, type, spacing, and contrast to help people understand what matters first.

Consistent components and states make interfaces easier to learn and easier to maintain.

03
◎

Design for real people.

Readable content, clear feedback, and accessible choices should be part of the design—not afterthoughts.
